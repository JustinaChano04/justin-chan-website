Product designed to reduce social anxiety by helping groups anonymously choose nearby restaurants and rank the most selected option.

## Overview

Picki was designed as a group decision-making product centered on restaurant selection. The core experience allowed users to vote anonymously within a group, helping people make plans without the pressure that often comes with social coordination.

## Product Goal

The idea behind Picki was to reduce the friction and anxiety that come with choosing where to go as a group. Anonymous input creates a lower-pressure experience while still producing a clear, ranked outcome.

## Outcome

The project won 2nd place at the Boilermake Hackathon.

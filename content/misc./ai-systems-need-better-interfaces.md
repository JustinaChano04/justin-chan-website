Building good AI products is not only about model quality. The interface around the system often determines whether the experience feels useful, trustworthy, or frustrating.

## Why this matters

- A strong model can still feel weak behind a confusing workflow.
- Clear interaction design improves confidence and usability.
- Product quality often comes from reducing friction, not only adding capability.

## What I care about

I am especially interested in AI systems where technical quality and product clarity reinforce each other instead of competing.

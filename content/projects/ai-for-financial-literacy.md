Leveraging GenAI to create an application that improves financial literacy through search, summarization, and identification of financial terms.

## Overview

This project explored how AI could make financial information easier to understand for everyday users. The core idea was to combine search, summarization, and term extraction into a single experience that reduces friction when people encounter unfamiliar financial concepts.

## Focus

- Use AI to surface and explain financial terms in plain language.
- Summarize dense financial information into more approachable takeaways.
- Make learning feel interactive rather than static.

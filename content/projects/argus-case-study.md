Case study for a smart doorbell and camera security system focused on facial recognition, motion awareness, and mobile notifications.

## Product Concept

Argus is a camera-based home security system designed to give users more assurance about what is happening at their doorstep. The concept includes facial recognition to identify family members and strangers, notification alerts through a mobile application, and tools for users to verify or add new faces over time.

## Problem Space

The smart doorbell market is growing quickly as home safety and family security become higher priorities. Argus was framed as a product that could use AI-driven recognition to make home monitoring more personalized and more useful than a basic motion alert.

## High-Level Goals

- Improve home security through smarter detection and recognition.
- Alert users when motion is detected at the door.
- Help users understand whether the person at the door is family, a delivery worker, or someone unknown.
- Support a personalized mobile experience for recognition and notifications.

## Success Metrics

- User acquisition and download rate.
- Engagement with the recognition feature.
- Daily and monthly active usage.
- Notification open rates.
- Customer satisfaction and usability feedback.
- Competitive comparisons and feature testing through user research and A/B testing.
